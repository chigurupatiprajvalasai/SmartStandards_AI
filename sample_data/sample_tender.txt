TENDER: PROCUREMENT OF LOW VOLTAGE ELECTRICAL CABLES

The department intends to procure PVC insulated copper electrical cables for building installations.
Cables shall be suitable for a nominal working voltage of 1.1 kV and shall have flame-retardant
properties. The supplier shall provide applicable test reports, inspection documents and certification.
The tender should specify applicable Indian Standards for the product, conductor and electrical wiring
installation. The purchaser may reject material that does not conform to the applicable requirements.
